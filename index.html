PromptShield

BCIT RESEARCH / IN DEVELOPMENT

A research prototype that checks untrusted content for instructions aimed at an AI assistant. The goal is to identify indirect prompt injection before that content reaches the model.

Research scope +

The work covers webpages, emails and documents, with a focus on classifying suspicious instructions and producing structured input. It is an ongoing research project, not a production security service.
