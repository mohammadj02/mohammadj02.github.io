Curiosity,
with evidence.

Effectiveness of Human-Crafted vs AI-Generated Phishing Attacks is a collaborative research project exploring which phishing messages people find more convincing.

The study examined responses from 50 participants to 12 phishing emails. Its findings challenge the assumption that AI-generated phishing is automatically more persuasive.
